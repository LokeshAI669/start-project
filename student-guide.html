🌟 Section 1

What is ProjectHub?

🚀 A Platform Built for Students

ProjectHub is a full-stack web platform that connects students with an admin/mentor team. It handles everything from project discovery to meeting scheduling — all in one place.

  • Browse a catalog of ready-made project ideas
  • Submit custom project requests with your own ideas
  • Schedule a 1:1 meeting with the team
  • Track the status of every submission in real time

🚀 Section 3

How to Use — Step-by-Step Guide

1

Create Your Account

  • Go to the Home Page and click "Get Started" or "Sign Up"
  • Fill in your Full Name, Email address, and Password
  • Click Register — you'll get a Welcome Email instantly ✅
💡 Tip: Use a real email address — all notifications (accepted, denied, meeting details) are sent there!
2

Log In to Your Account

  • Go to the Home Page and click "Login"
  • Enter your email and password
  • You'll be redirected to your Student Dashboard (/dashboard)
ℹ️ Note: Forgot your password? Click "Forgot Password" on the login page. A secure reset link (valid for 1 hour) will be sent to your email.
3

Explore Your Dashboard /dashboard

Once logged in, your dashboard shows four stat cards:

🟡
Pending
Awaiting admin review
🟢
Accepted
Check email for meeting
🔴
Denied
You can reschedule!

📌 Use "New Request" to submit • "Projects" to browse catalog • "Refresh" for latest status

4

Browse the Project Catalog /browse

  • Click "Projects" in the sidebar
  • Browse 100+ pre-defined project ideas across ML, AI, Web Dev, Cyber Security & more
  • Use filters to narrow down by type, technology, or domain
  • Click any project card to see full details
💡 Tip: Found a project you like? Click "Request This Project" on the detail page — it pre-fills the request form for you automatically!
5

Submit a Project Request /request

The request form is a 3-step wizard:

🔵 Step 1 — Your Info

  • Full Name (auto-filled if logged in)
  • Email address (auto-filled if logged in)

🟡 Step 2 — Project Details

  • Project Name — Give your project a clear title
  • Budget — Enter your budget + select currency (₹, $, €, etc.)
  • Description — Explain what you want to build
  • Attachment (optional) — Upload a reference file (PDF, image, doc)

🟢 Step 3 — Schedule a Meeting

  • Preferred Date — Pick a date from the calendar
  • Preferred Time — Choose a time slot (AM/PM)
  • Review your details and click "Submit Request"
⚠️ Important: After submission, you receive two emails: (1) A confirmation email to you, (2) A notification email to the admin team.
6

Track Your Request Status

Go back to your Dashboard any time to see the current status:

Submitted → Pending → Accepted ✅ (Email with meeting time) → Denied ❌ (Email with reason) ↓ Reschedule → Re-submit
🟡 PendingWait — admin is reviewing your request
🟢 AcceptedCheck your email for meeting date & time details
🔴 DeniedRead the reason in the email, then reschedule
7

Reschedule a Denied Request

  • Open your Dashboard
  • Find the denied request
  • Click "Reschedule"
  • Update the date/time and resubmit
  • ✅ Admin receives a notification with your updated request

💡 Section 6

Pro Tips for Students

💡 Be detailed in your description — the more context you give (tech stack, purpose, deadline), the faster your request gets approved.
💡 Browse the catalog first — there may already be a project matching your needs. Requesting from the catalog auto-fills the form, saving you time.
💡 Check your email inbox (and spam folder) after every action — all status updates are sent via email.
💡 Pick a realistic meeting time — choose a date at least 2–3 days ahead to give the admin team time to review.
ℹ️ You don't need to be a tech expert to use this platform. Just describe what you want to build in plain language — the team will handle the technical planning.
❓ Section 7

Frequently Asked Questions

❓ Do I need to pay upfront?
No. You only provide a budget estimate. Payment is discussed after your request is accepted.
❓ Can I submit multiple projects?
Yes! You can submit as many project requests as you want. Track them all from your dashboard.
❓ What if I forget my password?
Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page. A reset link will be emailed to you within minutes.
❓ Can I edit a request after submitting?
If your request is denied, you can reschedule and update details. Pending requests cannot be edited — contact the admin via email if urgent.
❓ Is my data safe?
Yes. The platform uses JWT (JSON Web Token) authentication. Admins and students have completely separate, secure access.
