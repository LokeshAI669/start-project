📝 Submitting a Request

A simple 3-step wizard

Step 1

Your Info

Enter your name & email. Auto-filled if you're logged in — no extra typing needed.

Step 2

Project Details

Project name, budget (₹ or any currency), description, and an optional file attachment.

Step 3

Schedule Your Meeting

Pick a preferred date from the calendar and a time slot. Then hit Submit — done! ✅
