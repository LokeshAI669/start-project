🔑 Feature 1 — Registration & Login

Create your account

1

Go to the Homepage & Click Register

Visit start-project-mu.vercel.app → Click the Sign Up button. Enter your full name, email address, and a secure password.

2

Check Your Email — Welcome Message Arrives

After registering, you immediately receive a welcome email confirming your account with your login details. Check your inbox!

3

Log In Anytime — Forgot Password? No Problem

Use your email & password to log in. If forgotten, click Forgot Password → a secure reset link is sent to you (valid 1 hour).

📁 Feature 2 — Browse & Submit a Request

Find your project, submit it

A
📁

Browse the Catalog

Go to /browse. Filter by category — ML, Web, Cyber, IoT, etc. Click any project card to see full details.

B
📋

Or Bring Your Own Idea

Click Custom Request. Describe your own project concept — even rough ideas are welcome!

Step 1
✍

Your Info

Your name & email are auto-filled if you are logged in. No extra typing needed.

Step 2
📝

Project Details

Enter project name, description, budget (any currency), and optionally attach a file (PDF, doc, etc.).

Step 3
📅

Schedule Meeting

Pick a date from the calendar and choose a time slot. Hit Submit — you are done! ✅
